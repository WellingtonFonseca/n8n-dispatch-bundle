# n8n dispatch: Dispatches screen, Resend and Timeline cards

The Dispatches screen (menu, filters, details), Resend, and the Timeline card for pending, cancelled and rescheduled steps.

## Dispatches screen and resend

A row of an Email sent through Mautic's mailer shows the state Mautic reports in the callback column, a different card and its own resend (only when the mailer refused it): wiki/n8n-dispatch-email-direct.md. An HSM handed to the DialogHSM plugin has its own card, callback column (Sucesso / Falhou) and resend (only what the plugin refused): wiki/n8n-dispatch-provider.md. Everything below describes the n8n rows.

Own entry of the main menu ("N8N Dispatch" > "Disparos"). Route `mautic_n8ndispatch.dispatch_index` (`/s/n8ndispatch/dispatches/{page}`), `Controller/DispatchController`, view `Resources/views/Dispatch/list.html.twig`. Needs **Campaigns > Full** (`campaign:campaigns:full`, `DispatchController::PERMISSION`): the menu, the screen, the details modal and the resend all ask for it. The poll's "Check now" needs `plugin:plugins:manage`.

## Timeline card for pending, cancelled and rescheduled steps

A step with an hour/day restriction holds the contact until the next valid window (normal Mautic behavior; do not mistake it for a broken integration: core re-checks it every cron cycle, and the contact's Upcoming Events tab shows the real time). The plugin's card (`_email_send.html.twig`, the registered `timelineTemplate`) fully replaces core's `Timeline/index.html.twig`, so it must carry core's pending-state block itself:

```json
{
  "data": "core already sends every pending row to the Timeline as a 'campaign.event.scheduled' entry (CampaignBundle LeadSubscriber, LeadEventLogRepository::getLeadLogs()): item.isScheduled and item.triggerDate (a real DateTime already reflecting the restriction). The card copies core's isScheduled block (text + reschedule/save/cancel buttons), shown only while metadata.n8ndispatch (the real dispatch payload) does not exist yet.",
  "|purify": "core's 'cancelled' message has no |purify (its 'scheduled' sibling does); the plugin's copy has it. Padrão: [ui-lista-card.md](ui-lista-card.md)",
  "colors": "core's .text-warning (#FDB933) is illegible on the card's white background: a scoped amber override (.n8ndispatch-timeline-scheduled .text-warning). Padrão: [ui-lista-card.md](ui-lista-card.md)",
  "reschedule field": "the date field core builds on Reschedule (#timeline-reschedule, campaign.js) has an INLINE `color: #000000` and no background: black on a dark card. n8ndispatch.css overrides it in the dark and solarized-dark themes (Padrão: [ui-lista-card.md](ui-lista-card.md)).   "buttons": "the card uses its own flex row (.n8ndispatch-timeline-scheduled, .n8ndispatch-timeline-actions); the hooks core's campaign.js selects by (.btn-reschedule, the save button id, the onclick handlers) are passed through `attributes`. Padrão: [ui-botoes.md](ui-botoes.md)",
  "cancel confirmation": "Mautic.showConfirmation called from the button's onclick (must stay a <button> for its disabled state; Padrão: [ui-modal.md](ui-modal.md)). Assets/js/campaign-timeline-scheduled.js.",
  "audit (who and when)": "core's cancel and reschedule endpoints record nobody and dispatch no event, so two plugin ajax endpoints are called ALONGSIDE them: recordScheduleCancellation (from the confirmation callback, right after Mautic.cancelScheduledCampaignEvent) and recordScheduleReschedule (a global jQuery ajaxSuccess listener filtered to core's campaign:updateScheduledCampaignEvent call, because a reschedule can be saved by Enter in the date field or by the plugin's Save button).
```
