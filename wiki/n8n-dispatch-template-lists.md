# n8n dispatch: the status column of the SMS and HSM template lists

The Status column (icon badge that opens on hover, Ativar / Desativar in the row menu) of the SMS and HSM template lists. It follows core's toggle icon of the row. The twin of the list badge of MauticPatchesBundle, duplicated on purpose: [../../wiki/ui-status-column.md](../../wiki/ui-status-column.md), [../../wiki/ui-badge.md](../../wiki/ui-badge.md), [ui-badge-n8n.md](ui-badge-n8n.md). The alert log of the Alertas tab reuses its CSS under the class `.n8ndispatch-alert-status` (check / cross icons): wiki/n8n-dispatch-alerts.md.

## What it does
